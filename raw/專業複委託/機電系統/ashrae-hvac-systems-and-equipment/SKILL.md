# American ASHRAE HVAC Systems and Equipment

## Overview

Professional HVAC engineering knowledge based on the **ASHRAE Handbook—HVAC Systems and Equipment**.

Applicable to:

- HVAC System Selection
- Central Plant Design
- Air Distribution Design
- Terminal Unit Selection
- Controls & Automation
- Engineering Education

# Workflow

1. Define System Requirements from Load Calculations
2. Select Air Distribution System Type
3. Select Central Plant Equipment Type
4. Size Air Handling Equipment
5. Size Terminal Units
6. Design Duct & Pipe Distribution
7. Select Pumps, Fans & Motors
8. Develop Control Sequence of Operations
9. Evaluate Life-Cycle Cost & Redundancy
10. Finalize Equipment Schedule & System Documentation
