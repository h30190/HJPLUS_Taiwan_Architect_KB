# Workflow

1. Define Product & Storage Requirements
2. Determine Design Temperature & Humidity
3. Calculate Product Load
4. Calculate Infiltration & Miscellaneous Loads
5. Determine Total Refrigeration Load
6. Select Refrigerant & Cycle Type
7. Size Compressor, Evaporator, Condenser
8. Design Piping & Controls
9. Evaluate Defrost Strategy
10. Evaluate Energy Performance & Safety Compliance
