# American ASHRAE HVAC Applications

- Building-Type-Specific HVAC Design

# Workflow

1. Identify Building/Facility Type
2. Review Applicable Code & Standard Requirements
3. Define Occupancy & Special Environmental Requirements
4. Evaluate Facility-Specific Design Criteria
5. Determine System Type Suitability
6. Assess Smoke Control & Life Safety Needs
7. Evaluate Energy & Renewable Integration Options
8. Develop Operations & Maintenance Plan
9. Plan Commissioning Requirements
10. Document Design Basis for Facility Type
